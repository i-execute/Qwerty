## Scanned documents

Several official fee orders had no usable text layer. A visual read may yield a number, but report it only when the programme, academic year, applicant category, and amount are plainly legible. Otherwise state `не удалось надёжно извлечь сумму из официального скана` and give the admissions contact. Never replace it with an aggregator value.
