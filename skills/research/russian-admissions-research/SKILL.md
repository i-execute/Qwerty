# Russian University Admissions Research

Use for current-cycle research on Russian university admissions, especially paid places (договор/платные места), deadlines, tuition, entrance thresholds, and admissions-office contacts.

## Core standard

1. **Treat the requested date as a snapshot.** State the as-of date prominently and compare every deadline against it. Do not call an intake “open” merely because enrollment or contract signing is still underway.
2. **Use official university sources as the primary evidence.** Prefer the current admissions landing page, then linked rules, deadlines, tuition orders, seat counts, and examination documents. Cite the direct official URL for every material claim.
3. **Separate applicant categories.** At a minimum, distinguish:
   - Russian citizens / persons equated with them;
   - foreign citizens applying to a Russian-language programme;
   - foreign citizens applying to an English-language or otherwise separate programme;
   - applicants who already filed documents versus applicants seeking to file a new application.
4. **Separate admissions milestones.** Report document-submission deadline, internal-exam deadline, contract deadline, and enrollment-order date as different facts. Explicitly label the current status: `new application possible`, `only contract stage for prior applicants`, or `closed`.
5. **Never confuse minimum scores with a passing score.** Minimum score is the threshold for admission to competition; the competitive/passing score is historical or depends on the current list. Say which one is being provided.

## Efficient official-source workflow

1. Begin at the university’s official domain. Identify the admissions page through site navigation or sitemap/robots metadata if site search is weak.
2. Extract direct links from the admissions page. Look for 2026 PDFs named or labelled: `Правила приема`, `Сроки проведения приема`, `Стоимость платных образовательных услуг`, `Перечень вступительных испытаний`, `Количество мест`.
3. Read linked PDFs directly. Extract text and inspect the surrounding context of the exact programme code (e.g., `31.05.01`) and relevant category headings.
4. Check that tuition has the right population and year. Fee documents often give separate prices for Russian citizens/equated applicants and foreign citizens.
5. Verify contacts on the admissions office’s own page, and distinguish them from a university-wide call centre.
6. For a claim that another university is still open, require explicit official proof that the **document deadline is after the requested date**, or that an officially published additional intake applies to that programme/category. Do not infer availability from a generic admissions page or from a contract deadline.

## Reporting template (Russian)

- Start with a short verdict/table: university, programme code, status on date, document deadline, tuition/year, minimum scores, contacts.
- Add programme and applicant-category qualifiers in the same row or immediately below it.
- Provide a concise section **«Неопределённость и что подтвердить звонком»** for unavailable/ambiguous fees, category differences, or unverified additional intakes.
- If external search is unavailable or blocked, say so briefly, but still inspect accessible official university sites and do not fill the gap with aggregators.
- Do not claim an exhaustive national list unless you actually searched a defensible official universe. Instead phrase results as **«подтверждённые варианты»**.

## Pitfalls

- A deadline page can state that documents closed while a later contract deadline remains. This means no new application; it does not establish that intake is open.
- **Verify the education level on every PDF before using its dates.** A university admissions site can link documents for bachelor/specialist programmes, residency (ординатура), master’s and postgraduate study from adjacent menus. A 2026 deadline in an ординатура PDF is never evidence of a deadline for `31.05.01` (specialist programme).
- **Do not turn a discovered admissions page into a confirmed alternative.** If the requested filtering condition is “deadline on/after [date]”, list a university as viable only after locating the document-submission deadline for the exact programme and applicant category. Otherwise put it under `неподтверждённые варианты` with the missing field explicit.
- Pages may silently redirect old domains to a new official domain. Cite the final canonical official URL.
- University website footers or navigation can show a prior copyright year; trust the date/version in the specific 2026 admissions document, not the footer.
- Search-engine blocks are not evidence that there are no other options.
- Do not present an unverified tuition figure or an inferred score as fact; mark it unavailable and give the admissions-office contact.
